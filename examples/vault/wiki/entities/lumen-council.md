# Lumen Council

The Lumen Council is an invented body that compares sensor logs from harbor stations. It appears in both fixture sources.

## Role

The council runs the tide based sort described in [[sources/tidal-sorting|Tidal Sorting]] and the reconciliation described in [[sources/quiet-protocol|Quiet Protocol]]. Its working assumption is that a stable shared record matters more than a fast one.
