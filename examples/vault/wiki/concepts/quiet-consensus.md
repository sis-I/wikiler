# Quiet Consensus

Quiet Consensus is agreement reached without broadcasting every intermediate vote. It is the central idea of the invented protocol in the fixture.

## Why it matters

The approach lowers traffic between stations. The final record still carries the information that [[concepts/marker-decay|Marker Decay]] would otherwise hide.
